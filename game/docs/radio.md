# NYC After Hours radio dial

Nine fictional stations share the in-game dial. Blue Hour’s 91.5 is its fictional dial position. Artist references describe the sound. Add playlist picks under each station as artist — title.

## Custom stations

### Radio Recital

**Classical piano** — A solo piano dial, from a quiet prelude with the sustain pedal down to a concert étude that fills the car.

### Grass Fields Community Radio

**Dreamy indie and shoegaze** — Charlie Martin, Lily Chou-Chou, Pasteboard and Electron Sheep set the mood. Start close enough to hear the singer breathe, then let the guitars swell until the voice nearly disappears.

- _Artist — first track_

### Rizz Assist Pirate Radio

**SoundCloud underground rap** — xaviersobased, kuru, che and Ken Carson. Leave room for both the blown-out bass tracks and the ones that sound like a half-remembered melody through phone speakers.

### The Supper Club

**Italian favorites and hot swing** — An Italian supper-club set that starts with a crooner or an accordion melody. When the band kicks in, go for Cuphead-style cartoon jazz, sprinting brass lines and big-band swing.

- _Artist — first track_

### Dark Knight Afterhours

**Batman-style night drives** — Nirvana, Kxllswxtch, INTERWORLD, Anton, The Neighbourhood, Pastel Ghost, The Weeknd and Daft Punk. A low guitar riff, a distant vocal or a repeating synth pulse should all feel at home on the same rain-dark Gotham street.

- _Artist — first track_

### 67 Radio

**Viral phonk and Brazilian phonk** — The big viral phonk cuts, with Brazilian phonk and funk edits at the center. Listen for clipped vocals, hard percussion and the drop everyone recognizes from the first beat.

- _Artist — first track_

### Party FM

**Mainstream rap** — Drake, Future, Jay-Z and Kanye West. Let a melodic hook lead into an Atlanta trap beat, then bring in a sample-heavy anthem with a verse the whole car knows.

- _Artist — first track_

### Blue Hour 91.5

**Vocal and cool jazz** — Frank Sinatra and Chet Baker set the pace. A conversational vocal over brushes can hand off to a muted trumpet ballad. Save room for the silence at the end of the phrase.

- _Artist — first track_

### Sugar Rush FM

**Bubblegum pop** — Feng, Suzy Sheer, Contact Sports and Lucy Bedroque are the references for this station’s take on bubblegum pop. Give the dial a rush of bright hooks, pitched vocals and playful electronic production.

- _Artist — first track_

## Phone and car radio design

The planned Radio app and the car tuner share one player. On foot, open **Phone → Radio** to pick a station by name and genre. In a car, use the dashboard’s previous and next station controls to tune while driving. Both show the station name and the current artist and track.

Tuning on either device updates the other. Closing the phone or entering or leaving a car keeps the current track at the same position. Either device can turn the radio off. Until a station has playable audio, show its name and “No audio yet.”
