# Paid game and optional Borough Cash

**Pricing recommendation for playtesting:** US$9.99 for the game and US$1.99 for a pack of 250 in-game dollars. These are proposed US prices. The publisher chooses the final amounts and regional prices in Steamworks. The playable candidate has local money and no real-money checkout. The [private purchase preview](preview.html) opens in a browser and lets the team try the confirmation and cancellation screens without an account.

| Base price under consideration | When it would make sense |
| --- | --- |
| $6.99 | The launch is primarily city exploration with a small social lobby and a short activity loop. |
| **$9.99** | Friends can reliably meet, explore and race, and the player enjoys multiple jobs and purchases during a full evening of play. |
| $14.99 | Playtests establish that players regularly return for deeper progression or shared activities. |

The base price includes all five boroughs, current jobs and the multiplayer lobby. Test the game with friends without offering paid currency first. Observe which item players save for, how long they enjoy earning it and where they lose interest. Measure local willingness to pay for the base game separately from interest in extras. Valve provides regional price proposals and handles developer-submitted package pricing. [Steam pricing](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/pricing)

## First purchase

**Borough Cash** adds 250 in-game dollars to the player's eligible online balance. At the current $80 base pay for a kitchen shift and $120 for a finance shift, this is roughly two to three shifts before quality bonuses. Test US$0.99 and US$1.99 for that same quantity in separate pricing interviews. Use one unambiguous amount and price in the eventual storefront. Show the localized price, exact grant and eligible spending before the player opens Steam Wallet. The design calls for guaranteed-value purchases, with purchased funds excluded from scratchcards, wagers, transfers and cash-out. Retain free play and earned income. [Current shift rewards](../../dist/work.js)

For the first post-launch content, consider direct purchases of an original outfit collection or a vehicle appearance pack. A later membership would need a predictable schedule of benefits people actually want every month. Rockstar sells currency and combines recurring currency with access to vehicles and other benefits in GTA+. Use original names and artwork for Concrete Jungle's offerings. [Rockstar’s GTA+ benefits](https://www.rockstargames.com/gta-plus/benefits)

## Production checkout

1. Authenticate the player through Steam and verify their ticket and app ownership on the publisher's backend. Associate entitlements with that SteamID across Windows, macOS and Linux. The anonymous friends lobby keeps working independently. Browser players would need a verified account-linking flow before accessing purchased balances on the web. [Valve authentication](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/auth)
2. Keep SKU, exact grant and localized prices on the backend. Determine the player’s Steam currency using GetUserInfo. Create a unique 64-bit order ID and persist player, SKU, quantity, price, currency and grant before calling InitTxn. Keep order IDs as strings across JSON boundaries. Store the publisher key only on the server. The Steam-distributed client opens Steam Wallet's authorization UI. [Implementation guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/microtransactions/implementation)
3. Upon approval, validate that the order belongs to the signed-in player, then finalize server-side. For uncertain results, query Steam and reconcile the order. Write the confirmed grant and balance change in one database transaction with a unique grant per order. Cancellations grant nothing. A disconnect, duplicate callback or server restart must preserve a single grant. Show a pending receipt to the player until the result is known. [Steam transaction API](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/webapi/ISteamMicroTxn)
4. Make paid spending authoritative too. Each spend has a unique ID, an eligible catalog item and a database transaction that debits the paid balance and grants the item. Restore entitlements after reinstall. Preserve existing local saves and locally earned cash. Editable local cash, the ephemeral lobby nickname and a copy of localStorage are unsuitable inputs for a paid balance. Handle refunded, partially refunded and charged-back orders with a recorded reversal and support-visible history. [Valve reconciliation guidance](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/microtransactions/implementation)
5. Use Valve's ISteamMicroTxnSandbox with the real AppID and approved partner access. Verify approval, cancellation, expired orders, duplicate notifications, retries, outage after finalization, insufficient balance, ineligible items, reinstall and refunds. Confirm cross-platform restoration. Select **In-App Purchases** on the public store page when the tested production feature is available. The UI preview exercises only the proposed player experience. [Steam microtransactions](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/microtransactions) · [Sandbox API](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/webapi/ISteamMicroTxnSandbox)
